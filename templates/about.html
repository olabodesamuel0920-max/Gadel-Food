About Gadel Foods

Our story, vision, and mission to make foodstuff shopping easier for FUTA students.

The Story Behind Gadel

We realized the challenges most students face when they are sent money to get foodstuffs:

  • What to buy: Difficulty in selecting essential foodstuffs that fit their budget.
  • Where to buy: Finding high-quality foodstuffs at fair student rates.
  • Logistics stress: Expensive transport fares to local Akure markets and the physical load of carrying heavy sacks.
  • Time constraints: Demanding lectures and coursework limit market shopping hours.
  • Exam season shortages: Foodstuffs finishing towards exam periods destabilize students' focus.

These were the real problems we spotted, and they formulated our vision and mission to serve FUTA students.
